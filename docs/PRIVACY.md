# Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 22, 2026
Version: 20260322

This Privacy Policy describes how Capybara Corporation ("iCTRL", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information about you when you access or use the iCTRL software, website, APIs, and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy forms part of our [Terms and Conditions](/terms).

We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), and where applicable, Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and Quebec's Act Respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector (Law 25).

## 1. Definitions

"Personal information" means information about an identifiable individual, as defined under PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy legislation. It does not include the name, title, business address, or business telephone number of an employee of an organization.

"Customer Data" has the meaning set out in our Terms and Conditions.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account information**: name, email address, password, and organization name when you create an account
- **Profile information**: role within your organization and preferences you set in your profile
- **User-generated content**: inventory item names, descriptions, labels, transaction logs, scanner inputs, exports, and any other content you create within the Service
- **Communications**: messages you send us through the contact form on our website

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage data**: pages visited, features used, actions performed, and timestamps of activity
- **Device and connection data**: browser type, operating system, IP address, and device identifiers
- **Cookies and tracking**: see Section 7 for details

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Authentication**: Supabase handles account authentication on our behalf; we receive your email and any metadata you provide during sign-up or invitation
- **Payment processing**: Stripe processes payment information; we receive transaction status and billing metadata, not full card numbers
- **Analytics**: PostHog provides analytics infrastructure; see Section 7 for details

## 3. Purposes for Collection, Use, and Disclosure

We collect, use, and disclose personal information only for the following identified purposes:

- creating and maintaining your account and organization within the Service;
- providing, operating, and improving the Service;
- authenticating you and enforcing access controls (sign-in, role-based permissions);
- sending transactional emails related to your account (invitations, billing, password resets);
- processing subscriptions and managing billing through Stripe;
- tracking usage events to understand how organizations use the Service (via PostHog);
- responding to your inquiries submitted through the contact form;
- detecting, investigating, and preventing misuse, fraud, or security incidents;
- complying with legal obligations, including breach notification requirements; and
- protecting the rights, property, or safety of Capybara Corporation, our users, or the public.

We do not use your personal information for marketing, advertising, or profiling purposes. We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Consent

By creating an account and using the Service, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy. Where we rely on implied consent, we do so only for purposes that a reasonable person would consider appropriate in the circumstances.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at <<contact@capyco.ca>> or by closing your account, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawal of consent may affect our ability to provide the Service to you.

Where the Service is used by an organization, the organization is responsible for obtaining any necessary consents from individuals whose personal information is submitted to the Service.

Information within your organization (such as inventory data and transaction logs) is accessible to other members of your organization based on their assigned role. We are not responsible for how your organization handles that data internally.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this Policy. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at <contact@capyco.ca>.

We retain certain information for longer periods where necessary for legal, regulatory, or legitimate business purposes, including:

- **Transaction and billing records**: retained for a minimum of seven (7) years for tax and accounting purposes, as required under the Income Tax Act (Canada) and applicable provincial legislation
- **Security event logs**: retained for a minimum of ninety (90) days
- **Terms acceptance records**: retained for the duration of your account plus two (2) years after deletion

After account deletion, we delete or anonymize your personal information within thirty (30) days, except where longer retention is required or permitted by law. Deletion of data held by third-party providers is subject to those providers' respective data retention and deletion policies.

## 7. Cookies and Analytics

### 7.1 Cookies

We use a limited number of essential cookies required to authenticate sessions and maintain security. These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function and cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality.

We do not use cookies for advertising, cross-site tracking, or third-party marketing purposes.

### 7.2 PostHog Analytics

We use PostHog to collect usage events and understand how organizations use the Service. PostHog collects:

- pages visited and features used
- device type, browser, and operating system
- approximate geographic region (derived from IP address)
- session duration and interaction patterns

PostHog is configured to:

- not collect or store full IP addresses
- respect Do Not Track browser signals
- anonymize or hash identifying information where feasible
- disable analytics collection if the PostHog API key is absent from the environment configuration

Analytics data is used solely to improve the Service and is not shared with third parties beyond the PostHog infrastructure.

## 8. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256 where supported by our infrastructure providers)
- access controls and least-privilege principles within our infrastructure
- Row Level Security (RLS) policies in our database to enforce organization-level data isolation
- regular security reviews and monitoring

No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security, and we are not liable for breaches that occur at our third-party infrastructure providers (see Section 5.2 and our Terms and Conditions, Section 7).

## 9. International Data Transfers

iCTRL is operated from Canada by Capybara Corporation, based in Calgary, Alberta. Our third-party service providers (listed in Section 5.2) process and store data primarily in the United States.

If you are located outside of Canada, your information will be transferred to and processed in Canada and the United States, which may have different data protection laws than your jurisdiction. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer.

In accordance with PIPEDA Principle 4.1.3, we use contractual or other means to require that third-party processors provide a comparable level of protection for personal information while it is being processed. However, personal information stored in foreign jurisdictions may be accessible to law enforcement and national security authorities of those jurisdictions under their laws.

## 10. Breach of Security Safeguards

If we become aware of a breach of security safeguards involving personal information under our control that creates a real risk of significant harm to an individual, we will:

- report the breach to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and, where applicable, the relevant provincial privacy commissioner, as required by law;
- notify affected individuals as required under PIPEDA and applicable provincial legislation; and
- keep a record of the breach in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Where a breach originates at a third-party infrastructure provider, our obligation is limited to forwarding any notice or information we receive from that provider and cooperating reasonably with affected parties. We are not responsible for the breach response obligations of third-party providers.
